ABOUT FYNDER

Built around the right connections.

Whether a child is travelling to or from school, a corps member is moving during the service year, or a passenger is travelling from one city to another, FYNDER provides connected safety tools designed to help people stay informed, protected and supported.

FYNDER brings people, safety information, emergency communication, location services and authorized response systems together in one connected platform.

OUR MISSIONUse technology to make education, national service, transportation and everyday movement safer for everyone.
OUR VISIONA connected society where people can access safety information, communicate during emergencies and receive timely assistance.
